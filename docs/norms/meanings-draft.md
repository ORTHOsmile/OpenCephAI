# Clinical meanings for each measurement — DRAFT for the owner

**What this is.** One plain clinical word-group for each direction of every live measurement in the catalogue
(`packages/analyses/src/catalogue/*.ts`). "High" means the patient's value is above the norm's mean; "Low" means below.
The app will put a severity word in front (Mild / Moderate / Severe) or "tendency" after, so each term is written to
read as "Mild <term>", "Moderate <term>", "<term> tendency" (ruling MEANING-2; bands in `SEVERITY.md`).

**Approved by the owner, 16 Oct 2026 (MEANING-APPROVE): all 90 rows, as the default starting wording**, on condition that
each clinician can change the words and keep their own in Settings (being designed). Was: nothing appears until ticked.

**Direction was worked out from each entry's own expression and `sign` rule**, not from memory: which line, which
direction, and which way is plus. Where the catalogue carries a page-cited sign rule (Jacobson, Kim, Ricketts, McNamara,
Downs, Holdaway via AlBarakati), that is named in the Source column as the direction's evidence.

**Sources the owner named (checked 16 Oct 2026):**

- **StatPearls, "Orthodontics, Cephalometric Analysis"** (Ghodasra & Brizuela), NBK594272 — **now read in full**, from
  the owner's saved copy of the PDF (`reference/norms/local/statpearls-cephalometric-analysis-NBK594272.pdf`, read 16
  Oct 2026; not on NCBI, and not in git). It covers ten of the ninety rows, by name or exact construct: SNA, SNB, ANB
  (Anteroposterior Evaluation), Wits (Wits Analysis), MMPA (Vertical Evaluation), U1–NA mm and L1–NB mm (Incisor
  Position: Linear Evaluation), and U1–NA°, L1–NB° and L1–GoGn° (Incisor Position: Angular Evaluation). Every row it
  covers **agrees** with the draft's High/Low direction; none differs. The other eighty rows are not discussed in this
  article at all (it is a short overview, not a full textbook chapter) and are marked "not in StatPearls" below. The
  PDF has no visible page numbers (it renders as one continuous page), so the Source column cites the section heading
  only.
- **Pocket Dentistry, "Cephalometrics and facial esthetics: the key to complete treatment planning"** (ch. 26) —
  **reachable, but paywalled after the first lines of "Lateral cephalometric assessment".** The free part has
  definitions only ("Reference lines, angles, and planes": occlusal plane, facial axis crossing Ba–N at a right angle,
  palatal plane) and no high/low meanings. Those definitions are cited where they bear on a row's construct.
- So **every meaning word itself is still from standard orthodontic usage**, now checked against StatPearls for the
  ten rows it covers (all agree) and marked "not in StatPearls" for the rest. The owner's tick is still the check for
  every row.

## Third reading: the owner's health AI (16 Oct 2026, uncited)

Same direction as this draft on almost every row, including #6, #12, #14, #15, #19, #42, #53, #58. Where it differs,
the app's own construct settles it in the draft's favour:
- #84, #85 pharynx: it said above = narrow airway; the value is the airway's width, so above = wide (draft stands).
- #43: it said above = Class III; S–N ÷ Go–Me higher = mandibular body short relative to S–N (draft stands).
- #13: it called Jacobson's sign a reversal; the app quotes Jacobson's own rule (direction agrees anyway).
- #30 vs #31: it called them conflicting; its own rows agree (above 90° = less vertical growth).
- #27: it read + as "posterior drop"; Kim's + is front-down = clockwise (draft stands; term itself flagged unclear).
**Owner rulings, 16 Oct (MEANING-3):** #16/#17 keep "non-extraction / extraction pattern"; #86 keep "obtuse / acute
nasolabial angle"; #33–#38 keep Jarabak's usual meanings. (Were: wording choices #16/#17 ("non-extraction / extraction pattern" vs "Class III / deep-bite pattern");
#86 ("obtuse / acute nasolabial angle" vs "retrusive / protrusive upper lip"); #33–#38 (keep Jarabak's usual reading,
or show no meaning word); #61, #64, #88 uncertain to it, kept per the app's sign rules.

**Rotation words (owner ROTATION-1, 16 Oct):** "clockwise" and "counter-clockwise" are as seen with the patient's right
cheek towards the viewer and the face pointing right (as the logo): clockwise = the front end down, counter-clockwise =
the front end up. The words describe the anatomy (front end up or down), so they hold however a film is shown; by default the app
flips left-facing films to this view (Flip can undo it), and the values themselves do not change with Flip.

**Two things true of every row:**

1. The word describes the patient **against the chosen norm**, not in absolute terms. An ANB of 1° against a norm of
   3° ± 1.5° reads "Mild Class III" even though many would call 1° Class I. That is the ruler, not the word.
2. "Mesiofacial" is the within-normal word and is never a high or low meaning, so it does not appear in the table.
   "Brachycephalic" is not used: no catalogue entry measures skull width (lateral film only).
