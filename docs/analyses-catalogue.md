# CephTrace — measurement catalogue (all analyses)

Agreed with the owner in chat and on the shared page, 6 Oct 2026. Appendix to `2026-10-06-analyses-and-norms-design.md`.

All 15 analyses can be built from one list of 92 measurements, each defined once; 9 choices below need your ruling first. This page is about **what is measured**. Norms come next, on their own page.

## How to read this page

- **One catalogue, many analyses.** Each measurement (for example SNA) is defined once: its points, its line, which angle, which sign. An analysis is a named list of catalogue measurements. NUS is built the same way as Steiner.
- **Same name, same thing.** Where two analyses use the same measurement, they share one definition. Where they truly differ (Steiner's U1–NA mm from the labial surface, the data on file's from the tip), they get two entries.
- **Source marks** on every measurement:
  - **P** = the analysis's own paper, on file and read.
  - **T** = the textbook (Naini, *Facial Aesthetics*, Ch. 7).
  - **S** = second-hand only (data on file, or a later paper following the original). Built, but marked "not checked against the original" in the app.
  - **?** = a choice or conflict for you (section 1).
- **Sign** means which way is plus. It is written out wherever a measurement can go negative.
- Comment on any line, or edit it in place. Nothing is built until you approve this page.

**How the app will behave (your rule, 6 Oct):**

- **Automatic:** an analysis whose every point an installed model finds is filled in on import.
- **Partly automatic:** any other analysis fills in the lines it can and says which points no installed model finds, e.g. *"Needs points no model finds: U1A, L1A. Place them to complete IMPA, FMIA, U1–NA°."* The guided steps then ask for those points.
- The check is live: install a model that finds more points and more analyses become automatic, with no change to the analyses.

**Which way is "down", whichever way the face points (FACING-1, corrected 12 Oct 2026).** Five lines above are drawn at right angles to FH or to the occlusal plane (N-perpendicular, A-vertical, PTV, Holdaway vertical, and the perpendiculars inside Wits and overbite). A right angle can be turned two ways; the app turns it so it runs **down the film** — from the forehead towards the chin — whether the patient faces right or left on the film. Picture it as a plumb line hung from N at right angles to Frankfort: it always hangs towards the chin, never up into the skull.

- *What it decides.* Four measurements use that line as "down": the sign of the palatal plane to FH (#27, Kim's "downward and forward = positive"; also inside #14 APDI, #15 ODI, #16 CF and #17 Extraction Index, which add it), the sign of the cant of the occlusal plane (#61, Downs), the sign of overbite (#69: + when the upper incisor tip is below the lower one), and where "3 mm below A" sits for basic upper-lip thickness (#77) and upper-lip strain (#79, which uses #77).
- *What was wrong before 12 Oct.* The app stores points as they lie on the film and turned the right angle the same way every time. On a film where the face points right that ran down; on a film where the face points LEFT it ran **up**. So on left-facing films #27, #61 and #69 came out with the wrong sign (e.g. palatal plane −0.88° read +0.88°), APDI/ODI/CF/Extraction Index were off by twice the palatal angle (≈1.4–3.5° on the test skulls), and the "3 mm below A" point was placed 3 mm ABOVE A, so #77 read 10.06 mm where the true value was 7.92 mm and #79 moved with it. Right-facing films were never affected: all 540 values on the six synthetic test skulls are identical before and after the fix.
- *Left-facing films saved before this fix show corrected values when reopened* — the app recomputes every number from the stored points; nothing stored changes.
- *What could still make it wrong.* Only a reference line that is exactly vertical on the film (FH or the occlusal plane standing upright), where "down" along a horizontal line has no meaning; the app then keeps its fixed turn. No real lateral ceph has an upright Frankfort plane. A test (`packages/analyses/src/facing.test.ts`) mirrors every synthetic skull and fails if any of the 93 measurements changes.

**Points this adds** to section 2, all placed by hand: a lower-border tangent point; Ricketts' Xi (built from four ramus points), Pm and DC; Pt and PTM (two different points); the back of the pterygopalatine fossa for PTV; the soft palate and pharyngeal wall points for McNamara's airway. Constructed points (CC, constructed gnathion, AO, BO, the A-vertical) are calculated, never placed.
